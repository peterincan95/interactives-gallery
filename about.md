## What is this project?

Over the last year I've been watching my friends and colleagues in the GIS and cartography world making some very cool projects on the web using AI coding tools. I've also been increasingly amazed (and increasingly unsettled) by the quantity and quality of AI-powered mapping and data-vis projects flooding my Instagram feed. 

With that in mind, I decided to take a month to start exploring this strange new world. I tempered my unease and started dipping my toes in vibe-coding some interactive maps. I've been using AI tools in my workflow for several years in a limited capacity; mostly limited to writing Python scripts for data prep and transformation and Arcade expressions for formatting popups and labels. For this project, I decided to take some maps I made in the past and turn them into interactive applications. The apps on this site are the result of this project.

I wanted to see if I could use these tools in a thoughtful way, and if I could use them to make things I could be proud of.

I'm still mulling over what I've learned and where I'm going to take things from here, but for now here are a few of my takeaways.
